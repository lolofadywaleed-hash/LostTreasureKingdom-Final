## Project-generated assets

The following files were generated for this project rather than retrieved from the third-party packs above:

- Audio: `assets/audio/fantasy_adventure_bgm.mp3`, `sword_hit.mp3`, `coin_pickup.mp3`, and `arcane_skill.mp3`.
- UI icons: `assets/ui/attack_icon.png`, `jump_icon.png`, and `skill_icon.png`.
- Grass texture: `assets/textures/forest_grass.png`.

These are original generated project assets, not sourced from the CC0 packs listed above; use remains subject to the applicable generation service terms.

## Existing user-supplied project media

The hero portraits under `assets/characters/` and Louay's model `assets/models/louay_rigged_animated.glb` predate this visual-upgrade batch and remain unchanged. Their ownership and permissions are governed by the original project/user-supplied materials.
